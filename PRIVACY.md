# Privacy and local processing

PhotoFindry performs tagging, face processing and search inference on the Mac.
Original photos remain in their folders. The library stores tags, names, previews,
embeddings and processing history locally. No mandatory account, advertising
analytics or app telemetry is part of the product.

Explicit model downloads and update checks contact their respective hosts.
Website and Buy Me a Coffee links open a browser on request. Those actions must
not upload photographs, names, tags, embeddings or search queries. The hosts can
receive normal connection metadata such as an IP address and have their own
privacy practices.

With required models available, local workflows are designed to work offline.
This is not a claim of enforced network isolation: the current process boundary
is not an OS network/filesystem sandbox. Observe the final candidate's network
behavior and qualify offline operation before publishing its release record.

Source availability or a valid signature alone cannot guarantee security. The
app and engine are proprietary; third-party licences/notices are included in the
application. Users retain access to read and export their own saved work.

Problem reports are voluntary. Review diagnostics for private filenames, queries
and photo details before sharing. Do not attach photo libraries, model weights,
original photographs or raw private traces to public reports.
