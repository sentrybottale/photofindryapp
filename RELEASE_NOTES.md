# PhotoFindry 0.2.0 beta 7 (build 87)

- Blocked tagging jobs now offer Reconnect originals directly in the library and Jobs. PhotoFindry checks the affected folder, asks for confirmation when needed, and continues that job after a successful reconnection.
- Cancelling a folder review leaves your work paused and lets you retry. A later Pause or Stop prevents automatic continuation.
- Model Start is available when a job is waiting for originals. A manually started model stays ready while the queue is idle instead of immediately stopping.
- Saving an offline gallery preview during tagging no longer incorrectly fails the result as a changed photograph.
- Model controls follow the current job state without refreshing the whole gallery.

This update replaces the application only. Your library, original photographs and selected model folders are preserved. Uncertain or changed originals still require review; the app does not silently confirm them.
